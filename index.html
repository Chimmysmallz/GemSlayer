❓ HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Basics: Tap or swipe two side-by-side gems to swap them. Line up 3+ matching gems to clear them and score.

✦ Match 4 in a row → Line Blaster (↔ / ↕) — clears a whole row or column.

🌈 Match 5 in a row → Color Bomb — clears every gem of one color.

💥 Make an L or T shape → Bomb Gem — blasts a 3×3 area.

Booster combos — swap two boosters together:

✦+✦ Cross Blast • 💥+💥 Mega Bomb • ✦+💥 Giga Cross • 🌈+gem Supernova • 🌈+🌈 Board Wipe

⚡ Blasters (buy in Shop): Hammer · Shuffle · Bomb · Bolt · Prism. Tap one, then tap the board.

👾 Boss levels cap each world — drain the boss's HP before your moves run out!

🟦 Jelly levels — clear the blue jelly off every tile by matching on top of it before moves run out.

💖 Lives refill over time · 🎁 Daily rewards grow with your streak · ⏱️ Timed mode feeds the weekly 🏆 leaderboard.
